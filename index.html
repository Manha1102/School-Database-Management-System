About Manha E-Solutions

A tax consultancy that also builds software, based in Jaggayyapet, NTR District, Andhra Pradesh.

We started as a tax and accounts practice, handling GST, income tax and payroll for local clients. Along the way we kept building the tools our own work needed, and then built the same for schools and petrol stations around us.

That is why our software gets the accounts right: it was designed by people who file returns and close books every month.

We work with clients in person, in Telugu or English, and keep supporting them after the software goes live.
